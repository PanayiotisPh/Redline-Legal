2. Accuracy

Redline measures using the sensors in a consumer phone. Those sensors have real limits, and we would rather state them than imply a precision we do not have:

  • GPS speed and position carry error, particularly in tunnels, urban canyons and poor signal.
  • G-force is derived from an accelerometer and depends on how the phone is mounted. It is accurate to roughly ±0.1 g.
  • Elevation is derived from air pressure, which is affected by weather.
  • Power estimates are calculated from acceleration and the weight you enter. They estimate power at the wheels, are not a dyno measurement, and will read below a manufacturer's crank figure.

Do not rely on Redline where accuracy matters: for legal, insurance, competitive or safety purposes. Figures are provided as-is for your own interest.
