Deleting your data

Last updated: 8 September 2026

From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open Redline and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete profile.
  4. Confirm. Deletion happens immediately: there is no waiting period and no way to undo it.

Without opening the app

Email privacy@redlinegforce.com from the address on your Redline account and ask us to delete it. Include the email you sign in with so we can find the right account. We will process the request and confirm by reply, typically within a few days and no later than 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, immediately:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Every trip you recorded: routes, speed, distance, and G-force data
  • Your profile: display name and avatar
  • Any group you created (which also removes it for everyone in it), and your own membership in groups you joined

Nothing is kept in a "soft-deleted" or archived state after this runs. The deletion is a direct, permanent removal from our database, not a flag that hides the data while keeping it.

What is not affected

  • Other members' own trips and stats in a group you were part of are unaffected: deleting your account removes your data and your membership, not anyone else's.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as transaction records for a completed subscription purchase, which are retained only as long as applicable law requires and are not used for anything beyond that legal obligation.

Deleting only some of your data, without deleting the account

You do not need to delete your whole account to remove specific data:

  • Individual trips can be deleted one at a time, or in bulk, from the trip history list (long-press to select, then Delete).
  • Your avatar can be removed from Profile, which falls back to your initials.
  • Leaving a group removes your data from that group's leaderboard without touching your account or your own trip history.

These are also immediate and permanent. See the in-app confirmation on each action for specifics.
